# Gate 5 — Reviewer requirements

Status: requirements extracted from docs/PHASE0_ASSESSMENT_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md;
no assignments or approvals verified.

| Subject/role | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Science reviewer | Relevant science teaching or subject expertise; age/grade-appropriate explanations and misconceptions |
| Social Science reviewer | Relevant history, geography, civics, or economics expertise; sources, chronology, context, causal claims |
| Mathematics reviewer | Mathematics expertise; symbolic equivalence, units, tolerances, and valid methods |
| English reviewer | English/language expertise; meaning, paraphrase, context, and objective-relevant grammar |
| Adjudicator | Named independent decision-maker for disputed/high-impact records; records resolution and rationale |

Each record needs reviewer identity, qualifications/evidence, independence
check, assignment date, item-level checklist, source verification, version,
sign-off, and review date. Statuses nominated/contacted/agreed/started/
completed/approved/rejected must remain distinct. No current reviewer may be
assumed or invented.
